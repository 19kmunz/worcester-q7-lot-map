Allow single-family homes on 5,000 sq ft lots in Worcester

A proposed zoning amendment for the RS-10, RS-7 and RL-7 districts, following Worcester's 59.1% to 40.9% vote for Question 7 on November 3, 2026. (Election-night results.)

Two maps show two different things. Neither is a promise of construction.

MAP 1: WHAT COULD CHANGE

Lots that could be subdivided

3,991

new single-family lots could be created by subdividing existing large lots, beyond what today's area and frontage minimums already allow. This is potential for owners who choose to add a home on a large lot of their own. 68 more lots are marked as a potential data issue and are not counted.

Data: CSV · GeoJSON
MAP 2: WHAT ALREADY EXISTS

Homes already below the district minimum

3,937

existing detached single-family homes already sit on lots of 5,000 sq ft or more that are smaller than their district's minimum (10,000 sq ft in RS-10, 7,000 sq ft in RS-7 and RL-7). About 98% were built before 1991. A further 677 homes are on lots under 5,000 sq ft, which a 5,000 sq ft minimum would not reach.

Data: CSV

Map 1 looks forward: large lots that could gain another home. Map 2 looks back: homes already built on lots of this size. Parcel data: Community Scale Question 7 lots tool and MassGIS assessor parcels (FY2026). Zoning: City of Worcester. Counts are potential or descriptive, not forecasts: the data does not show buildings, wetlands or easements, and frontage is not tested on Map 2.
