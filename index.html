Allow single-family homes on 5,000 sq ft lots in Worcester

A proposed zoning amendment for the RS-10, RS-7 and RL-7 districts, following Worcester's 59.1% to 40.9% vote for Question 7 on November 3, 2026. (Election-night results.)

4,150 Worcester lots in those districts meet the 5,000 sq ft and 50 ft frontage test for at least one more home. 2,783 new lots would become possible beyond what today's minimums already allow.
